01Trellis · Decision intelligence

Dashboards tell you what happened. Trellis tells you what changed.

Trellis compares every day against baseline and forecast, then surfaces only the movement that matters: which branch, which channel, which item, and how material it is.

  • Role-based views for executives, growth and operations
  • Forecast overlays with confidence ranges
  • End-of-day, week and month briefings
  • A live signal feed that stays quiet unless something is material
