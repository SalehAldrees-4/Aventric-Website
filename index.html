Decision intelligence

Trellis

The central brain for multi-branch F&B operators. Trellis connects sales, branches, brands, channels, delivery platforms, menu performance, campaigns, and forecasts into one executive intelligence layer.

It does not simply show dashboards. It detects meaningful movement against baselines and forecasts, explains what drove it, and routes attention to where it matters — from end-of-day briefs to live business signals.

Food safety & traceability

Convoy

Every ingredient, accounted for. Convoy tracks food safety and traceability across the supply chain — from supplier to branch to plate — so compliance becomes a live signal, not a quarterly scramble.

When something needs attention, Convoy shows the full chain of custody in seconds: what moved, where it came from, where it went, and what to isolate.

More specialist intelligence modules are on the way — for growth, margin, and expansion. The ecosystem is just getting started.
