01Decision intelligence for restaurant groups

One loop, from financial targets to verified outcomes.

Trellis sits above the systems a restaurant group already runs. It turns their data into trusted business facts, then connects those facts to decisions, accountable actions and measured results.

  • One governed definition for every metric, with its coverage and freshness stated
  • Recommendations ranked by financial value, not by how unusual a number looks
  • Every action has an owner, a deadline and a measured outcome
  • A person approves any change to an outside system
  • Built around Saudi and GCC restaurant economics
